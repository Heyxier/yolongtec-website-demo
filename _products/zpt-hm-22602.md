# ZPT-HM-22602 Brushless Cordless Rotary Hammer

The ZPT-HM-22602 is a professional-grade brushless cordless rotary hammer engineered for concrete and masonry work. Powered by Yolong's proprietary brushless motor technology, it delivers 2.8J of impact energy for demanding jobsite conditions — from construction sites to renovation projects and daily maintenance tasks.

## Key Features

- **Powerful Impact** — 2.8J impact energy easily penetrates concrete and block
- **4-in-1 Functionality** — Switch between hammer, drill, chisel, and screw modes with one button
- **Extended Runtime** — 20V 5.0Ah Li-ion battery supports continuous operation
- **Lightweight Design** — Only 2.8 kg, reducing fatigue during extended use
- **Dust-Proof Structure** — Engineered for harsh construction environments
