# ZPT-CD-18502 Brushless Cordless Impact Drill

The ZPT-CD-18502 is a professional-grade brushless cordless impact drill engineered for the toughest jobs. Featuring an 85 N·m high-torque brushless motor, it delivers reliable performance for drilling into wood, metal, and masonry, as well as heavy-duty screwdriving applications.

## Key Features

- **High Efficiency Brushless Motor** — 30% more runtime and extended motor life
- **85 N·m Max Torque** — Handles demanding fastening and drilling tasks
- **All-Steel Gearbox** — Durable construction for professional daily use
- **LED Work Light** — Illuminates dark workspaces
- **Smart Battery Management** — Protects battery from over-discharge and overheating
